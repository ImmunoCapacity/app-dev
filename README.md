# My Favorite Movies

## Dead Poets Society

**Genre:** Drama
**Director:** Peter Weir
**Main Actor:** Robin Williams

> "Carpe Diem. Seize the day, boys. Make your lives extraordinary."

**Summary:**
A passionate English teacher inspires his students to think for themselves and live life fully. It’s about courage, freedom, and the power of words.

---

## Good Will Hunting

**Genre:** Drama
**Director:** Gus Van Sant
**Main Actors:** Matt Damon, Robin Williams

> "It’s not your fault."

**Summary:**
A janitor with a brilliant mind struggles with his past until a kind psychologist helps him understand love, trust, and self-worth.

### Why I Like Them

Both movies teach valuable lessons about life, learning, and believing in yourself.
